How scoring works

Each criterion is min–max normalized across the vans you currently have included (0 = worst, 100 = best; “less is better” criteria are flipped). Total score = weighted average over all criteria at your slider values. Missing values score 0 — fill data in before comparing hard. ⚠ = figures not yet verified against the spec sheet of those vans.

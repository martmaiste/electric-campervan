## Warranty

- **Vehicle warranty:** 2 years from first registration, no mileage limit (manufacturer).
- **Dealer extension:** after the manufacturer warranty expires, the dealer adds a 3-year extended warranty — 3 additional years or 150 000 km, whichever comes first. Does not cover the battery.
- **Battery warranty (manufacturer):** 8 years or 160 000 km, whichever comes first.
- **Mobilo:** roadside-assistance package, valid up to 30 years; includes 24 h road service and mobile service for longer repairs. **Corrosion warranty:** up to 12 years (both extended by each service). Details: mercedes-benz.ee/korrosioonigarantii-van, mercedes-benz.ee/MobiloVan

## What the list does NOT contain

- **Kerb weight** — only GVW (*täismass*); kerb weight in `data/vans.js` is still a launch-coverage estimate.
- **WLTP range** — only energy consumption; range in `data/vans.js` (410 km) is derived from 113 kWh ÷ 27.6 kWh/100 km (420e A3).
- **Overall / cargo dimensions, turning circle** — not listed; still launch-coverage estimates in `data/vans.js`.

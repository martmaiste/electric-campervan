>
> - **Market:** Finland — EUR; prices **Autoveroton (sis. Alv 25,5 %)** = excl. vehicle registration tax, incl. 25.5 % VAT.
> - **Valid from:** 28.8.2026 (*suosittuhinnasto* = recommended price list), prices valid until further notice; Ford delivery fees (*toimituskulut*) added on top.
> - **All models:** AUTO (1-speed) gearbox, RWD rear-wheel drive, Trend trim.
> - `*` range & consumption calculated per the WLTP test method; real-world values may deviate significantly (driving style, weather, pre-heating, heating use, speed, load, road profile).

> - N1 = GVW 3,500 kg (*350*) · N2 = GVW 3,900 / 4,250 kg (*390* / *425*).
> - `hv` = horsepower. Battery figure as printed: N1 table says "netto", N2 table says "brutto/netto" — the marketed 68/96 kWh is used throughout.

## Standard equipment (*vakiovarusteet*) — VAN Trend / extended-cab VAN Trend

Warranty: **Ford warranty 5 y / 150,000 km or 2 y without km limit** · **HV battery & components 8 y / 160,000 km**.

Key items (both variants; differences noted):

- **Exterior:** halogen headlights, DRL, front fog lamps, body-coloured front bumper (partial), rear mud flaps; sliding door right-hand side; H2 roof on L2/L3, **H3 roof on L4 models**; side marker lights (L4); 200° rear doors with 90° stop (standard); **270° rear doors with magnetic stops on L4 models**; 16" steel wheels (D2XAD) with full-size wheel arch covers; heated/electric mirrors (N1: non-folding, N2: folding).
- **Cargo area:** cargo tie-down loops, cargo light, **composite partition with window** (standard).
- **Interior:** VAN: Seat Pack 8 (1+2 heated front seats, fabric, 4-way manual, lumbar, armrests, table on middle seat); extended cab: Seat Pack 7 (1+1) + 3 second-row seats; lockable glovebox, sunglasses holder, roof storage console, electric front windows.
- **Infotainment:** Finnish SYNC 4 with voice recognition, **12" touchscreen**, navigation (standard N2; extended cab always), radio/Bluetooth/wireless Android Auto/Apple CarPlay, 4 speakers, **5G modem**.
- **Technology:** auto high beam, auto wipers, keyless start, 1 remote + 1 standard key, BLIS blind spot monitor (N2), ESC + Hill Start Assist, AGM battery (N1: 1× H7 80 Ah; N2/extended cab: 2× H7 80 Ah), **automatic climate control with fresh-air filter**, lane keep (N1: lane keep only; N2: lane keep + lane following), central locking, seat belt reminders/pre-tension driver, heated windshield, front + rear parking sensors, heated steering wheel, **electric parking brake**, TPMS, AEB 2.2 (camera + 2 radar, N2) / AEB (camera, N1), ACC + lane following + Stop&Go (N2) / cruise control (N1), wrong-way warning, intelligent speed limiter.

*Anomaly:* the extended-cab standard list contains "Polttoainetankki 70 litraa, AdBlue-lisäainesäiliö 24 litraa" (fuel tank 70 l, AdBlue 24 l) — a diesel-spec leftover; BEVs have no fuel/AdBlue tank.

## What the list does NOT contain

- **Cargo dimensions** (length/width/height) and **overall dimensions / wheelbase / turning circle** — not in the document.
- **Kerb weight** — only GVW (*kokonaispaino*) and payload (*kantavuus*); kerb ≈ GVW − payload (payload includes 75 kg driver).
- **DC fast-charge power** — only the Mode 3 cables and the 2.3 kW ProPower Onboard output are listed.
- **Top speed** — not stated.
- **96 kWh on chassis** — chassis only exists with 68 kWh.
- **Towing for N2** — shown as "–" (N1 350: 750 kg braked).
